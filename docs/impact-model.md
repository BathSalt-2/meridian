# MERIDIAN — Impact Model

> Designed to be measured. Impact data is owned by communities, not extracted from them.

---

## Philosophy

MERIDIAN tracks its own impact. All indicators listed here are measured within the LOCUS knowledge graph and are available to communities through their MERIDIAN dashboard. Sharing impact data with program evaluators, researchers, or funders is always community-controlled — never automatic.

The impact model is designed to demonstrate value at three levels: individual farmer, community collective, and regional ecosystem.

---

## Individual Farmer Indicators

### Agricultural Productivity

| Indicator | Measurement Method | Baseline Source |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| Seasonal yield outcome | Community-reported post-harvest assessment | LOCUS Outcome nodes |
| Disease/pest intervention success rate | IRIS severity staging pre/post intervention | LOCUS Observation + Intervention nodes |
| Early warning lead time | Days between PRAXIS alert and observable outbreak | LOCUS Alert + Observation timestamps |
| Resource expenditure per unit yield | Community-reported cost vs. yield outcome | LOCUS Intervention + Market nodes |
| False positive alert rate | Alerts issued where no threat materialized | LOCUS Alert + Outcome nodes |

### Knowledge System Engagement

| Indicator | Measurement Method |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Observation frequency | IRIS sessions per month per community member |
| Knowledge graph growth rate | New nodes per season |
| Traditional knowledge entries | Elder knowledge capture sessions completed; entries active |
| PRAXIS recommendation follow-through | Intervention nodes linked to PRAXIS alerts vs. total alerts |

## Community Collective Indicators

### Resilience Outcomes

| Indicator | Measurement Method |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Season survival through stress events | Post-stress-event yield outcomes vs. pre-MERIDIAN baseline |
| Food security self-assessment | Community-administered survey at season end (stored in LOCUS) |
| Alternative income activity uptake | LOCUS tracking of PRAXIS economic recommendations acted on |
| Pre-threat preparation completion | Actions completed before PRAXIS-predicted threat events |

### Multi-Device Network Effect

| Indicator | Measurement Method |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Community monitoring coverage | Percentage of registered land areas with observations in last 30 days |
| Sync frequency | Average sync events per device per week |
| Shared observation volume | Observations contributed by non-primary-device community members |

## Ecological Trajectory Indicators

### Land Health

| Indicator | Measurement Method | Time Horizon |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------|
| Forest cover change rate | IRIS sequential landscape photography | Monthly |
| Soil health trajectory | IRIS soil indicator trend across seasons | Seasonal |
| Water source reliability | Seasonal drying/recovery timing vs. historical | Seasonal |
| Erosion severity trend | IRIS erosion staging over time per Place node | Seasonal |

### Biodiversity Proxies

| Indicator | Measurement Method |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Acoustic species richness | Distinct species signatures detected per acoustic session |
| Beneficial insect presence/absence | IRIS acoustic and visual identification |
| Invasive species encroachment | IRIS landscape identification |

---

## Carbon and Ecosystem Service Metrics

For communities participating in carbon or PES programs:

| Metric | Methodology |
|--------|------------|
| Forest carbon stock proxy | Forest cover area × regional biomass density factor |
| Soil carbon trajectory | Organic matter indicator trend as proxy for carbon sequestration direction |
| Watershed service indicator | Water source reliability trends upstream/downstream |
| Biodiversity unit proxy | Acoustic species richness × habitat integrity score |

All metrics are exported with PSALM ZK-proof certification for use in formal program documentation.

## Regional Intelligence Contribution

For communities sharing data through the PSALM mesh:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|-----------|------------|

| Inter-community knowledge exchange events | PSALM sync events with knowledge commons contributions |

## Long-Term System Health

| Indicator | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Seasonal active users | >80% of registered community members active each growing season |
| LOCUS retention rate | <5% of communities abandoning MERIDIAN after first full season |
| Traditional knowledge growth | Positive growth in traditional knowledge entries each season as trust builds |
| PRAXIS accuracy trend | Declining false positive rate over time as LOCUS historical depth increases |
